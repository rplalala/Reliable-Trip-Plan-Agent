## Minimum daily coverage (2026-09-25)

The shared post-generation product diagnostic now distinguishes `satisfied`, `missing`,
`exempt`, and `unknown` per requested day. One countable primary visit satisfies the
minimum; 2-5 remains default improvement guidance. V1/V2/V3 count valid supplied canonical
main visits; V0 preserves its explicitly labelled name proxy and performs no external
verification. No new concrete-experience identity contract is invented. Nearby, transfers,
free time and unbound generic activities do not satisfy coverage. A REQUIRED named cafe
can remain a main visit; a meal placeholder cannot inflate counts.

The existing single interpretation gains source-linked `TimeProtection.full_day` for
explicit whole-day rest, transport-only or other non-sightseeing commitments. Historical
missing values do not assert absence of restrictions. Unresolved relevant requirements,
unknown roles/identities and unsupported same-day remaining hours retain UNKNOWN.
An explicit full-day commitment or fixed intervals covering the entire established planning
window can exempt a day. Almost-full occupancy and relaxed pace are not exemptions; no
minimum visit duration is invented. Blank-day window creation is shared with the existing
09:00-18:00 policy and requires reliable destination timezone information.

V0/V1/V2 expose the diagnostic without acquiring Repair capability. V3 uses the existing
coverage finding with reason `minimum_daily_coverage_missing`, CONFIRMED product-policy
status and magnitude 1; this is not a provider safety/access fact. Hard confirmed
obligations precede minimum coverage, which precedes ordinary opt-in review goals. Scope,
candidates and route preparation operate on active targets only. Minimum completion is
0->1; enabled quantity review can subsequently pursue 1->2 on the originally authorized
date. Disabled quantity review does not disable the minimum. Component comparisons retain
other unchanged missing dates and cannot manufacture success from role or evidence changes.

No primary generation prompt or budget was changed. Routes remain 24 shared requests,
8 post-proposal reserved requests, 32 elements. Source, fixed-time, access UNKNOWN,
EXCLUDED/REQUIRED, overlap and mixed-route protections remain. An unresolved minimum is
returned explicitly with the partial itinerary, not fabricated or labelled objective
infeasibility. Product HTTP/UI shows missing/exempt/unknown coverage separately from Nearby.
Historical live records do not contain this rule. See `v3_development.md` for validation
chronology and the separately authorized live outcome.
